## StarGazing 1.0.1, crisp stacks and a fix for the Start crash

An early preview, like 1.0.0. Tested in the simulator and with automated tests, but **not yet on a
real iPhone**.

### What's fixed

- **Pressing Start no longer crashes.** The background task is now registered under its own
  identifier before it is submitted.
- **Stacks are crisp, not blurry.** Handheld photos are now lined up on their own detail, so
  buildings and edges stay sharp. The result starts from the **sharpest** photo of the set, not just
  the first. On a set of ten handheld city photos the stack kept about 93% of the original's edge
  strength and had about 3 times less noise in the sky.
- **Skies keep their mood.** The brightness stretch no longer turns a dark sky grey. It only lifts
  very dark skies, gently.
- **Photos come out the right way up** and in their original proportions.
- **Limited photo access is reported** clearly, and photos that can't be read are listed.
- **Interrupted runs can be resumed**, and analysis results are saved with each photo.

### Speed

Ten 36-megapixel photos now take about 100 seconds in an optimized build on a Mac (it was about 210
seconds before). The app does the work on all processor cores. Phone speed has not been measured.

### Files in this release

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `StarGazing-v1.0.1-unsigned.ipa` | The iPhone app, **not code-signed**. It can't be installed as it is. |
| `StarGazing-v1.0.1-simulator.zip` | The app for the iOS Simulator. |

Installing needs your own signing (Sideloadly or Xcode) and Developer Mode. See
[docs/RELEASING.md](https://github.com/imranhc0/starGazing/blob/main/docs/RELEASING.md).

### Known limitations

- Not tested on a real iPhone: speed, memory and background behaviour are unmeasured.
- Very large RAW batches will probably run out of memory.
- There is no mask brush and no stretch or denoise sliders yet.
- Background stacking is unproven; keep the app open while it works.
- Strong perspective or parallax can still blur a little; alignment is not per-tile.
- RAW decoding has not been tested against real DNG files.

Full details are in the [README](https://github.com/imranhc0/starGazing#known-limitations).
