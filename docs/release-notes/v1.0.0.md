## StarGazing 1.0.0, first preview

The first build of StarGazing: an iPhone app that stacks several night-sky photos into one clean
image. This is an early preview. It has been tested in the simulator and with automated tests, but
**not yet on a real iPhone**.

### What's in it

- **Stacks 5 to 200 photos** from your library: RAW (DNG), HEIC or JPEG.
- **Three modes:** *Clean sky* (less noise, sharp stars), *Median* (also removes planes and
  satellites that cross only some frames), and *Star trails* (stars become streaks).
- **Finds and lines up the stars itself.** Photos it can't use (clouds, too few stars, unreadable)
  are skipped and listed with a reason.
- **Keeps the ground sharp.** The sky is aligned on the stars, the foreground is stacked as shot, and
  an automatic sky mask blends them.
- **Picks up where it stopped.** Progress is saved after every photo; if a run is interrupted, the
  app offers to resume it.
- **Before and after** comparison with a draggable slider.
- **Saves to Photos** as a 16-bit TIFF for RAW photos, or a JPEG otherwise. Everything runs on the
  device.

Needs **iOS 26 or newer** (an iPhone 12 Pro or newer is the target).

### Files in this release

| File | What it is |

### Installing on an iPhone

The `.ipa` has to be signed with your own Apple ID or developer certificate before it will install,
for example with Sideloadly or Xcode. The phone needs **Developer Mode** turned on (Settings >
Privacy & Security). A phone managed by an employer, school or lender may not allow that. A free
Apple ID gives apps that expire after 7 days. See
[docs/RELEASING.md](https://github.com/imranhc0/starGazing/blob/main/docs/RELEASING.md).

### Known limitations

- Not tested on a real iPhone: speed, memory and background behaviour are unmeasured.
- Very large RAW batches will probably run out of memory.
- There is no mask brush and no stretch or denoise sliders yet, so a wrong automatic sky mask can't
  be corrected.
- Background stacking is unproven; keep the app open while it works.
- The foreground is not aligned, which suits a tripod but not handheld shots.
- RAW decoding has not been tested against real DNG files.

Full details are in the [README](https://github.com/imranhc0/starGazing#known-limitations).
